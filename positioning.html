Data-driven analysis of The Book Fairies’ fiscal and strategic standing among regional literacy nonprofits.

Comparative Metrics

Program efficiency and operating margin were derived from IRS Form 990 Part IX (Lines 13–25), providing uniform financial comparability.

Organization Program Efficiency (%) Operating Margin (%) Total Revenue (USD)

Reach Out & Read 85.2 0.8 $1,592,431
LINC (Literacy Inc.) 86.4 1.1 $5,022,611

Source: IRS Form 990 (2023–2024), ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, CauseIQ.

Positioning Map

Comparative placement by program efficiency (X-axis) and operating margin (Y-axis). The Book Fairies occupy the high-efficiency, high-surplus quadrant — indicating sustainable impact.
