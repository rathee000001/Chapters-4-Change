Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Turning Every Donor Dollar into Direct Book Ownership — The Most Efficient Literacy Nonprofit in Long Island and NYC.

1. Efficiency & Financial Stewardship

The Book Fairies dedicate 90.1% of expenses directly to program services (Form 990, 2023), outpacing peer nonprofits like Reach Out & Read (85%) and Literacy Nassau (82%). Book Fairies IRS 990 | Reach Out and Read | CauseIQ Data

2. Local Reach & Visible Impact

Serving Long Island and NYC, Book Fairies’ 5+ million books distributed ensure donors see tangible impact. 2023 Financials

3. Direct Ownership Model

“We believe in the power of book ownership,” states their mission. Every book distributed is owned by a child permanently. About Us | Mission

4. Equity-Driven Focus

Operating in under-resourced NYC and Long Island communities, Book Fairies advance educational equity through consistent, measurable literacy outreach. Book Fairies Homepage

5. CSR Partnerships & Scalability

Cerini & Associates LLP donates one book for every billable hour—over 250,000 books to date. Cerini Partnership | Book Fairies Partners | LinkedIn
