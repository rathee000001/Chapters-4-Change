Targeting

Evaluating donor segments to determine the most impactful and sustainable sources of funding for literacy-driven social change.

Evaluating Donor Segments

Book Fairies engages verified donor types including corporate sponsors, major donors, peer fundraisers, and in-kind contributors—each validated through reports and partnerships.

1. Corporate Partners / Sponsors

Corporate CSR partners like Cerini & Associates donate per billable hour, aligning high funding capacity with literacy-focused community initiatives.

2. Major Individual Donors

Financial statements reveal three major donors supply substantial annual revenue—indicating reliable, high-value support aligned with educational and social mission goals.

3. Event / Peer Fundraisers

Annual Read-a-Thon and online campaigns demonstrate strong peer-to-peer engagement, visibility, and social momentum for recurring sponsorship and donor retention.

4. In-Kind Book Donors / Drive Hosts

Community drives deliver high participation and mission fit, reinforcing literacy access though with lower monetary yield relative to sponsors.

Selected Target Market

Corporate sponsors and major donors represent the most strategic audience—offering high financial impact, mission alignment, and strong visibility in NYC region.

Mission Reinforcement

Both donor groups align financial support with literacy goals, ensuring equitable access to books throughout Long Island and New York City.
