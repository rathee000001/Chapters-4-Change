Target Market Appeal

How each audience contributes to The Book Fairies’ mission through shared literacy engagement and social impact.

Audience Overview

Each audience contributes uniquely to The Book Fairies’ mission while gaining educational and social value through the Chapters 4 Change campaign.

University Students & Faculty

Participate through campus events linking education and literacy. Students volunteer, promote reading equality, and drive community awareness.

Corporate & Alumni Partners

Corporate CSR teams and alumni provide financial and mentorship support—helping scale The Book Fairies’ literacy programs sustainably.

Local Families & Community Members

Families engage through book drives and local chapters, fostering an intergenerational reading culture across Long Island and NYC.

Broader Appeal

The campaign unites campuses, corporations, and families— diversifying donors while amplifying a shared purpose in literacy equity and community empowerment.
