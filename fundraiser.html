Campaign Title: Chapters 4 Change — A University Partnership for Literacy Equity

Objective

Launch Chapters 4 Change with NYIT, NYU, and St. John’s to raise $75,000 and collect 6,000 books for under-resourced communities across Long Island and NYC.

Campaign Concept

An interactive campus-driven movement linking literacy, mentorship, and community giving through on-campus events and digital storytelling.
