Past Fundraising Examples

Key fundraising campaigns that strengthened literacy engagement and expanded donor impact through creative, community-based participation.

Annual Read-a-Thon (Virtual Fundraiser Event)

The Book Fairies’ annual Read-a-Thon connects readers and donors through a digital literacy challenge — raising funds and fostering joyful reading.

The event used the Givebutter platform for peer-to-peer giving and visibility across schools and classrooms.

Challenge to Match 5 Million Books

A milestone matching campaign doubling every donor’s contribution — motivating supporters through transparency and visible literacy milestones.

The initiative inspired hundreds of donors by linking every gift to real-time book distribution impact across Long Island and NYC.
