# Chapters 4 Change - Strategic Marketing Website

Chapters 4 Change connects a campaign's purpose to the audiences, messages, channels and measures needed to act on it. The work combines a 15-page strategy presentation with a reviewable presentation-oriented website.

**[Open the live project](https://rathee000001.github.io/Chapters-4-Change/index.html)** · [Portfolio project story](https://ai-portfolio-kohl-beta.vercel.app/projects/chapters-for-change)

## What this project does

### Understand who the campaign needs to reach.

Four-dimensional audience segmentation and target prioritization distinguish people with different motivations, constraints and roles. The strategy connects audience needs to mission and available resources.

That prevents one generic appeal from carrying every part of the campaign. Segmentation is useful when it changes the value proposition, channel or action being proposed, not merely when it adds more audience labels.

### Make the positioning specific.

Audience-specific value propositions and positioning connect the mission to a reason to participate. The strategy considers channel roles, appeals and campaign materials as connected decisions.

Peer comparison provides context, but unsupported efficiency or 'most efficient' claims are not carried into this portfolio. The supported work is the reasoning structure and communication plan, not a validated ranking of nonprofit performance.

### Plan the campaign sequence.

Campaign phases, channel choices and a family of materials turn the strategy into a proposed execution path. Each channel has a purpose in moving an audience from awareness toward a meaningful action.

The work includes target priorities, donor/audience framing and measurement dimensions. Those are planning decisions: proposed figures are not measured fundraising, participation or conversion outcomes.

### Make the strategy reviewable.

The strategy became a 16-route presentation website with sequential navigation, print support and motion-aware interactions. The structure lets a reviewer follow the argument from mission through targeting, positioning and execution.

This is where communication design supports strategy: the site organizes the reasoning so it can be inspected and presented, rather than treating a polished landing page as evidence that a campaign succeeded.

## Scope and limitations

15-page strategy; 16-route presentation website. Campaign KPIs are planning targets, not measured fundraising outcomes.

Keeping the pages separate preserves the contribution of each: Chapters 4 Change explains the strategy; The Book Fairies demonstrates the visitor journey. Neither is presented as a verified fundraising operation.
