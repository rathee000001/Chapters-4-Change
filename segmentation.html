Segmentation

Understanding how The Book Fairies strategically reach, engage, and sustain diverse supporter groups across regions and motivations.

1. Geographic Segmentation

Operates mainly across Long Island and NYC metro areas, expanding trust and literacy outreach locally and internationally through community partnerships.

2. Demographic Segmentation

Donors include individuals, corporations, and foundations; over ninety percent of revenue derives from contributions by major and institutional supporters.

3. Psychographic Segmentation

Supporters value literacy equity, educational impact, and community accountability, aligning giving with measurable outcomes and shared social responsibility principles.

4. Behavioral Segmentation

Donors engage through monetary gifts, corporate sponsorships, in-kind book drives, and participation in recurring fundraisers like the annual Read-a-Thon.
